Setup Guide

  1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens and create a new token
  2. Give it data.records:read and data.records:write scopes, and access to your base
  3. Copy the Base ID from your Airtable base URL (starts with "app")
  4. Enter your table name exactly as it appears in the tab
  5. Click "Test Connection" to verify everything works
